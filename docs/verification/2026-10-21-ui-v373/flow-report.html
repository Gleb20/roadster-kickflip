Scope

Focused menu, pause, results and safe update boundaries. No gameplay, artwork or backend redesign.

Flow: play, pause, finish

  1. 1Menu

    Best score with actual or hypothetical global rank; centered Leaderboard; Install and Legend utility tiles.

    Primary: Let's Go → playing.

    Waiting release → safe reload then fresh start. Legacy best → hypothetical rank; no unverified submission.
  2. 2Pause

    Current score, Home top right, primary Resume with Restart to its right.

    Primary: Resume → same frozen attempt.

    Restart → fresh run, applying a ready update first when safe. Home → menu.
  3. 3Results

    Score and text Submit score act as one group; personal best and landed flips remain. Home top right; muted Try again below.

    Primary score action: Submit score → explicit arcade-name form, verification, accepted rank and sharing.

    Offline/local replay without an online session → no false submission button. A ready update waits while results or forms remain visible.
