Frames were decoded locally with AVFoundation; the private recording and its notification contents are not included in this release.

The near terrain now fills the transparent strip below the building silhouettes, so the midground cannot protrude underneath them. It still draws behind roofs and walls.

Audio: after explicit SOUND ON, use navigator.audioSession.type=playback where supported; release the prior session on mute. Initialize/prime WebAudio synchronously in touch/key gestures; resume both suspended and interrupted states. Preserve the first effect while async resume resolves, discard stale or muted requests, and reactivate on touch completion and resume. Read-only diagnostics report audio state/session. [WebKit explanation of silent-switch routing](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=237322), [interrupted AudioContext report](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=273511).
