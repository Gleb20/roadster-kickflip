# 3.9.3 Mobile input

22 October2026. Expanded THRUST hit target, all artwork and gameplay rules retained. Text/background of the tip now receive ordinary gameplay input; its cross independently dismisses it by touch or keyboard. Active field/thrust touchstart and touchmove cancel native long-press handling, with Pointer Events as the sole input path. Two fingers remain independent and keep their initial roles until release. Pause/Sound/menu interaction is preserved.

Collision calculations skip sweeps wholly outside the original horizontal acceptance window and calculate the same projection extrema without temporary arrays. No collision geometry, substep precision, route safety margin, RNG, physics constant, score, fuel or server ruleset change. No automatic slide-switch, duration floor or queued jump is introduced.
