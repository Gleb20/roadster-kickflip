## Controls and flight

Ground: hold screen / Space / W / Up to charge over 0.4 s, release to jump. Air: hold again to grab and backflip; release to extend the legs and brake the current rotation without automatic upright targeting. The first jump retains the old cosmetic kickflip, without a paid flip reward; the next airborne press switches immediately to manual rotation, and paid thrust also ends the cosmetic phase. No queued ground charge. THRUST / B / either Shift is held independently, including a second finger. Cancellation, blur and pause clear held input. Controls are available immediately.

Launch speed is 560 + 440 × charge, gravity 1550; spin max7.2rad/s, acceleration60rad/s². Release brakes at24rad/s² toward1.8rad/s in the existing direction; a stationary ordinary jump stays upright. Landing requires angle within ±40° and angular velocity at most 240°/s. A good wheel-aligned landing completes the final degrees within the landing tolerance, so a 358-degree landing is not undercounted. Timing sweeps verify that an uncharged tap cannot land a flip; quarter-charge has a narrow safe window, half-charge has a wider window, and charged thrust can land two. The threshold is physical, without a mechanic unlock.

Thrust acceleration is 2100 away from the underbody; its horizontal part has drag 1.8/s and a ±260 world-unit/s cap. Fuel: initial 50, capacity 100, burn 80/s, +25 per landed flip; no passive refill or ground burn. First thrust latches the car to the feet over 0.12 s for the remainder of the flight. During manual flips, vertical Roadster frame 0 and Elon share a pivot 65 above the tyre line. A0.12s tuck reaches the existing front-left grip; releasing opens over0.18s; the two 36-unit arm bones keep their lengths. The rotating body collision proxy follows the 32-unit crouch. The old nine-pose roll is reused for the first cosmetic jump and remains in the read-only review. Shared rotating collision proxies are an inset car rectangle and torso/head rectangle, tested against obstacle boxes using SAT and adaptive swept substeps. Air boxes have explicit altitude, top and bottom.

## Scores and speed

Distance gives floor(distance / 20), without multiplication. Passing an obstacle pays 50 × current multiplier immediately. Flight rewards settle only on a good landing: n flips pay 50n(n+1), late takeoff and close landing pay 25 each; ideal landing within±10° adds50, including an ordinary upright jump; sum uses the multiplier before landing. Each flip, ideal landing and each confirmed near event raises the multiplier by 0.1, capped at ×5. Multipliers use integer tenths. A bad landing cancels that flight's pending reward; previously passed obstacle rewards remain.

A late takeoff has ≤0.12 s until the ground obstacle's front collision edge reaches the car; it pays only after clearing that obstacle and landing. Close landing is ≤0.12 s of travel behind a ground obstacle's rear collision edge. No negative distances, no aerial landing bonuses, no repeat awards per obstacle/event. Compound events share one landing settlement.

Resultful actions refill the combo-support meter. Duration is 8 − 1.2(multiplier − 1) seconds; after expiry, lose 0.1 per 0.5 s. A landing with flips or a close landing adds +5 percentage points of speed once, capped at +25%; each refresh holds 4 s, then loses 10 percentage points/s. Meter decay, fuel, and earned speed are independent.

Speed feedback refinement, 18 October: earned speed is still a real +5% per resultful landing, capped at +25%; it moves obstacles, road and scenery faster. Camera framing follows only the normal progression pace, so temporary earned speed or stumble recovery is not compensated by zoom. Road travel is integrated in screen pixels each step, rather than multiplying all accumulated travel by the latest zoom. This avoids camera changes cancelling or reversing the road scroll. The feedback factor is the actual earned gain (1.00–1.25), limited by the net speed above base after directional thrust. Grounded boost lean is capped at 0.035 rad (about 2 degrees); the short translucent flame reaches at most 55 world units and 0.36 opacity, without the former broad halo. Six faint speed lines move with the road. Impossibru requires both the maximum 660-unit base pace and the full +25% actual earned gain (825 units/s, 500 km/h / about 311 mph). The first few tricks retain the ordinary face. Physical-device feel remains subject to playtesting.

A bad landing resets multiplier and earned speed, preserves remaining fuel, clears residual horizontal thrust speed and starts a seated recovery. Duration is 2.8 + 0.65 × clamp((pre-fall base speed − 360)/300, 0, 1) seconds. The rider leans back with two forward bent legs and raised arms. Seated contacts remain at14%,36% and56%: the first two produce low rebounds; the third directly launches a higher arc. Knees fold and the head, shoulders and hands compress relative to the torso at contact, then rebound. The rider stands in flight (68–84%) and lands on the car at97%, settling by the end. Jump and thrust are locked; separate primary taps add 0.15 playback rate, capped at 1.6×, without jumping or skipping poses. Even maximum tapping preserves at least 1.75 s of animation. Seated and car collision proxies remain active; no obstacle immunity. Car collision uses the measured convex side-view hull and swept checks include its translation/rotation. ## Progression and teaching

0–15 s low singles; 15–35 charged jumps/flips; 35–60 pairs/thrust practice; 60–90 hovering then incoming drones (incoming introduced after 72 s); 90–120 precision trains; 120–150 mixed/thrust trains; 150+ mixes the learned templates. Every fifth template is a low single with extra breathing room; a further breather follows any two consecutive complex templates. Recovery pauses difficulty. All abilities remain usable in the opening seconds.

Contextual, dismissible tips teach jump, charge, flip, thrust, air and near manoeuvres. Success persists in `roadster-coach-v3`; replay uses a quiet “Replay tips on next run” checkbox in the main menu, consumed by the next explicit start. Toggling it never starts a run. Each run resets temporary tip dismissal. No tutorial dialogs, ability gates, account requirements or external telemetry.

Each template starts from the actual grounded player/fuel/pace state and has a witnessed grounded exit. A pure simulator searches input schedules including charge duration, thrust, near rewards, speed streak and moving air objects. Generated routes must also survive the same policy with takeoff lead shifted ±35 ms; mandatory-thrust routes retain at least 20 fuel, and thrust trains are considered only with at least 65 fuel. It never mutates the live state. Templates do not overlap an unfinished predecessor. If no witness is found, try a verified low single; if that too is invalid, wait. Empty fuel always keeps a recoverable path. Positions are never expanded/teleported during speed changes; camera scale changes smoothly and resize preserves obstacle-relative positions.

Precision in 3.0.4: at most two low cars in the first group, one high hovering drone (altitude 400) and a later third car with a 1.15-second offset. The former repeated drone ceiling and narrow forced takeoff were removed after physical-device feedback. Thrust trains are capped at six cars. Mixed ground/air patterns remain, with witnessed timing margin and low-fuel fallback. Close manoeuvres still earn rewards; the generator no longer insists on the former exact precision-only route.
